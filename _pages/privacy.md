This site uses **Google Analytics 4** to understand how visitors use it — for example, which pages are viewed, roughly where visitors come from (country/city level), and which website or link referred them. I use this only to see which content is useful and to improve the site.

- Google Analytics sets cookies and collects data such as pages visited, device and browser type, and referring site. IP addresses are not logged or stored by Google Analytics 4.
- I do not use this data for advertising, and I do not sell or share it.
- Data is processed by Google under the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [how Google uses information from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).
- You can opt out with the [Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout), or by blocking cookies or using a content blocker in your browser.

Questions? Email [fung.yip@gmail.com](mailto:fung.yip@gmail.com).

Optional interaction analytics, when enabled and permitted, measures which portfolio links are selected, email or LinkedIn contact-link activation, resource-link activation, and a case-study reading proxy based on visible time and article depth. A contact-link click does not tell me that a message was sent. Custom events use bounded content and placement labels, not email addresses, message contents, account IDs, or named-visitor profiles.
